Word-for-word scripts for the calls and texts at the **end** of the recruiting
pipeline: the BOB (offer) call, the new-start run-up to orientation, and the
three ways someone is let go.

reproduced here. No tables or images in the original.

## The week around a BOB

1. Download 2nd-round applicants, upload them to the daily update.
2. Send the BGC link to everyone being BOB'd.
3. Do the BOB calls.
4. Update ApplicantStream and OBCL for every BOB call.
5. Send the recap text to the new start as soon as the call ends.
6. Post the "Daily Update Message" to Slack for the leaders.

**Four rehash points**

- A "Daily Update Message" Slack thread is started.
- Recruiter/owner follows up the next day with leaders who have not replied —
  DM, with tagging.
- **Friday:** recruiter does confirmation calls, updates OBCL, and starts a
  "New starts scheduled for Monday" Slack thread.
- **Saturday:** rehash from the leader.

### Message from recruiter to the team

> Hey Team! Can we text all the people hired and congratulate them for getting
> the position? Send them this pre-made text, and make edits with your name and
> their name; phone numbers are in the screenshot. Once the text is sent, please
> confirm with "sent" on this Slack thread.

### Message to the person BOB'd, from the leader

> Hey X, this is X, I talked to our hiring managers, and they told me that they
> decided to offer you the position. Congratulations on getting the position,
> and I look forward to working with you in the future!

## BOB call script

> Hey this is Rafael with Alphalete marketing, how are you?
>
> I was calling to chat about the 2nd interview that you had with X, do you have
> a quick minute to chat?
>
> Awesome, so first I wanted to check to see if you had any questions about the
> interview after you wrapped up?
>
> *X*
>
> Okay awesome, so the management training program, Pay, schedule, day to day,
> all of that made sense to you?
>
> Awesome, is there anything that they mentioned to you that you feel like you
> wouldn't be able to handle with the proper training?
>
> Okay awesome. Well after talking to all of our account managers, going through
> all the interviews that we had, X had a lot of great things to say about
> you………. They really went to bat for you……. So honestly, we think that you'd be
> a great fit and we would love to bring you on board!
>
> Of course! Congrats on the new position!
>
> **If they don't say thank you, say this:** *I'm assuming you're accepting the
> position?*
>
> So let's go through a few things before coming into orientation!
>
> First, we need everyone to have a passed background check in order to
> represent the client, would you have any issues passing the background check?
>
> Okay awesome, so I just sent you the background check. It's always a little
> hard to find in the email. The email will come from "the first advantage", do
> you mind checking your email to make sure you have it please?
>
> **If they say yes:** (You see where it says "get started"?)
> **If they say no:** (Can you check your junk mail please?)
>
> Awesome, it should only take about 7-10 mins to complete. Would you mind
> taking it by the end of day, because we need it to come back as soon as
> possible so we can get you badged up.
>
> Thanks, and once you fill it out can you send me a confirmation text message
> so that I can mark my system please?
>
> Thank you!
>
> Okay, and X told me when the soonest that you could start would be, but I
> don't have the paper in front of me, can you remind me again when would be the
> soonest you can start?
>
> Awesome, so we can actually do this Monday at this time, and it'll go till
> about 5 or 6pm at the latest, can you make that work?
>
> Okay cool, so just so you know the dress code is business professional, so
> everyone will be in a full suit and tie, do you have a full suit & tie?
>
> **If they say no:** Okay, do you think you would be able to get one before
> Monday so you can look the part?
>
> Okay, that'll be good so that you can look the part!
>
> That Monday, we're gonna go through expectations of the company, then a lot of
> product knowledge of the client we're representing, so you'll definitely want
> to bring a notebook and pen, sound good?
>
> Cool, I'll send you over all of this through text so that you have it in
> writing, if you need anything from me, please feel free to reach out to me!

### If they don't answer

> Hey X, This is Rafael Hidalgo. I was calling about the 2nd interview you had

## Parting ways

### Failed background check

**How to hold it.** This is not a conversation, it is a statement. The BGC
failed, there is nothing we can do, we have to part ways. Do not ask questions
or open it up to a discussion. Be short and quick. Be empathetic and wish them
the best. Put it on the client — nothing we can do, the client requires a
passed BGC.

> Hey This is X, with X Company, how are you?
>
> So we just got your BGC back, and unfortunately it's come back failed. Us as a
> company we don't have any issues with it, but our clients require a passed BGC
> to represent them, so unfortunately, there's nothing we can do, we're going to
> have to part ways.
>
> Sorry about that, we wish you the best!

**"What if I get my lawyer to write something up?"**

> So unfortunately a lawyer writing something doesn't change anything, the
> background check has to come back "passed". So if you want to talk to them and
> in the future they get rid of it from your record, then call us back and we can
> have you retake the background check. Sound good?

**"What if I get it expunged?"**

> For sure, the background check has to come back "passed", so if you get it
> expunged and it's off of your record, then call us back and we can have you
> retake the background check. Sound good?
