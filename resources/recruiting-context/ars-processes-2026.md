# Pestaña 2**  
**

**INTRODUCTION**

  

Hey everyone, hope you're doing great. My name is XXXX. I'm one of the hiring managers here at xxxxx. It's great meeting you today.

  

Just to let you know, our interview process is going to be really simple today. This is the first part of a two-step interview process. Just because I'm expecting about 40 candidates today, this is going to be more like a screening process. It's actually a chance for me to put a face on the resume that already caught our attention and, obviously, get to talk to you a little bit more and get to know about your goals and what you're looking for. I will also give you some information about our company and what we're looking for, and if those paths align, we can talk about moving into a second interview.

  

That second interview will be for sure one-on-one. That way, we will have more time to cover all the details of the role, the day-to-day. We will touch base on a lot of information about advancements and promotions, and obviously give more information about a schedule and pay, even though we're going to cover that information today too. **Any questions?**

  

Great, perfect, so we're going to start with a round of questions for everyone. It's going to be real simple questions. I'm going to ask everyone the same thing. We're going to go one by one. I'm not going to go with any particular order; it's just my screen’s order, but I want to give everyone their fair chance to talk, okay?

  

-----

**Q\&A**

  

  - Tell me a little bit about yourself, your working background and your expectations in this next roll. ?
  - If offered the position, how soon are you looking to start? 
  - What caught your attention about this role?

  

*(Now, you chose one question depending the candidate’s profile)*

  

  - *\*What’s one decision in your career so far that taught you the most about yourself?\** 
  - *\*When pressure builds up, what part of you shows up? emotion, logic or leadership?\**
  - *\*What makes you leadership material?\** 
  - *\*How do you bring out the best in others, not just yourself?\** 
  - *\*How do you handle days when your plan doesn't go as expected?\** 

  

-----

**COMPANY BACKGROUND - WHAT WE DO**

We are a marketing and advertising company, and we’ve been in business with our top client, AT\&T, for years now. 

As you may see, AT\&T handles the front-end - branding, marketing and promotions - while we manage the back end. 

We focus on marketing, sales, customer service, leadership development, and client relations. 

We manage their face to face interactions with potential clients to close sales and take care of the aftermath of it with a personalized customer service experience. 

So, of course, they are the face, but we are the skeleton, the infrastructure that keeps everything running smoothly. We’re looking for individuals with a strong work ethic, great communication skills, and a student mentality — someone who’s eager to learn and grow.

  

The positions we are looking to fill are in-person, full time, where you will be interacting with potential clients. All interactions with them are face to face, so we do not do call center or inside of a retailer type of work or anything like that.

  

We’re looking for an individual who can briefly start off at the entry level position, that way that person can learn the ins and outs of the business rather quickly. 

  

Ultimately we want to put someone into a Management role within 6-8 months to manage their own team and clients.  So, this is a very fast paced environment and with lots of growth opportunities. So we’re definitely looking for someone that can thrive in a fast-paced environment. **Does this sound aligned with what you are looking for?**

-----

**PAY STRUCTURE**

So now let's go over the pay. 

So for entry level team members we start with a weekly paycheck, this is a performance based-role with an average paycheck from $1000 - $1500 plus bonuses and commission. 

Now as soon as someone gets into that assistant manager position (this happens within the first 4-6 months of being with us) we move to a salary role, between 65k - 80k a year. 

At the Excecutive Manager position - that means you manage a whole office, team, your deal with clients and everything else - we are talkin about between $250k plus bonuses and commission plus the profits from the revenue. 

**Overall, is this a comfortable compensation rate for you?** 

-----

**SCHEDULE**

Regarding the schedule, all the positions we are looking to fill in are FULL TIME, IN PERSON, DAY SHIFTS. We are talking about a minimum of 40 hours a week, with the possibility of working on Saturdays to make more bonuses. **Is that alright with you?****  
**Remember that we are located in (CITY), **is that a sustainable commute for you for an everyday job?**

-----

**WRAP UP**

This does conclude the first interview. I’ll keep on talking with candidates today and we’re only going to select a few people for a 2nd interview. 

The 2nd round is an opportunity for the candidates to meet one of our account managers at our head office. 

If you are selected you’ll get a phone call before 5pm today from our recruitment team in order to schedule a 2nd interview. I would recommend dressing business professional attire for that and bringing a notebook and pen.

 if you don’t get a phone call it just means we went a different direction, but other than that it was great meeting you hopefully we talk again in the future, bye\!

# What if scenarios  

**What if scenarios**

**(answers and reactions)**

  

1.  **\*\*What if they ask what clients we work with?\*\***

Our main client right now is AT\&T but we also work with clients in different industries such us Verizon, Tmobile, Apple, google, Samsung  and others. 

1.  **\*\*What if they never turn on the camera even though we asked many times already and also indicated via zoom to turn on the camera?\*\***

In such cases, the best thing to do is to send them a request to start a camera and a private message via zoom chat. In case there is no response from the candidate by the time he/she has to answer the personal questions, you can remove him/her from the meeting. 

1.  **\*\*What if they ask about vacations?\*\***

In this case, the first thing to do is to quickly ask if they already have any kind of trip planned for the next 6 months (which is the duration of the onboarding process). 

If they say yes, we ask for the dates and tell them that we will notify the Head of Department so that they can take it into account in the future, in case we move forward with their profile. 

In case they do not have anything planned, we tell them that as every company, we have contemplated the rest of each team member, so their time off is something that can be discussed with their Manager when appropriate. 

1.  **\*\*What if they ask if it's a door to door kind of job?\*\***

“If D2D means to knock on random doors to sell something, we do not do that. We work with LEADS. That means that the potential customer leaves their information in the ATT (or Box) website, they send us that info and we schedule an sitting with them to assess their situation and recommend the most accurate service for them”

“If by D2D you to mean to knock on random doors to sell something. We do not do that. We do meet with Clients F2F, we have a special way that we get leads, almost warm and we’ll talk to typically 30 cx’s per day. So it is F2F, is that something you’re comfortable with?” 

  

1.  **\*\*What if they ask what a typical workday is like in the company? What is the day to day like ?\*\***

“Well, a typical day at the office, for the first 6 months of your onboarding will be divided between your training and your job. We want the onboarding to be more of a soft landing that prepares you for all possible scenarios and gives you time to get to know the culture of the company, our problem solving system, and how we handle our clients. The idea is that you start with training in the mornings and work in the afternoons in equal proportions at the beginning, and then you will spend more time working and less time training, until you are working at full speed and do not realize that your training is over, because you will feel naturally comfortable in the position.” 

1.  **\*\*What benefits do you offer? When do those benefits start? With the training or before that?\*\***

That's a great question for the second round of interviews.   
“As I discussed at the beginning of our zoom call, this meeting is more general and the answer to your question may vary depending on the position you are applying for, the level of responsibility of the position and other things. You should definitely ask the Head of Department in the next round, because they love to talk about company benefits.”

“So we actually offer full benefits once someones completes our management training program and we promote to Market manager” 

1.  **\*\*What if a candidate is vaping, lying on a bed/sofa? Would that be reason enough to disqualify them?  Should I remove them at that moment?\*\***

These cases must be treated with much elegance and we must remind them that, although we give them the benefit and comfort of having the first interview by Zoom, it is still a job interview, so there are certain behaviors that are not recommended. It is important in this case, to speak in general and see if the candidate takes accountability. If he/she does and apologizes, we say thank you for their apology and move on. If the candidate does not apologize, we continue with the interview and then disqualify him or her. As long as he/she is not being disrespectful or bothering the rest of the candidates, there is no reason to remove him/her. 

1.  **\*\*What if someone says that is a scam?\*\***

In these cases, the important thing is to act quickly. 

The first thing to do is to make it very clear that we are a serious company and do not take defamation lightly. 

If the person respectfully asks: “Okay, I understand your concerns because there are a lot of companies that start with ponzi schemes that ask you for a certain amount of money to enter the system. Here we don't do that,  Everyone is a W2 employee, in fact, the first few months we invest in you and your training with a fully paid Training Process where we teach you all the different areas of marketing and management to carry out our client's campaigns with the best standards in the industry. You can check our social media presence and our verified profiles for more peace of mind”.  
  

If the person is disrespectful and starts shouting and not allowing you to speak, they need to be removed immediately. After that, you need to apologize to the applicants that remain in the meeting. 

“I'm really sorry you had to go through that situation. A lot of times people choose not to listen to us and I have a good answer to that question, but he/she didn't want to listen to me: I understand you can have concerns because there are a lot of companies that start with schemes that ask you for a certain amount of money to enter the system. Here we don't do that, in fact, the first few months we invest in you and your training with a fully paid Training Process where we teach you all the different areas of marketing and management to carry out our client's campaigns with the best standards in the industry. You can check our social media presence and our verified profiles for more peace of mind”.

  
  

1.  **\*\*If someone asks about the onboarding or if you had it:\*\*** 

“The onboarding process includes the training period that we consider necessary to grow in this company. 

Those who join our company will be trained in the 5 key areas that I mentioned before in order to get to know how the company works, but also to see in which areas you feel more comfortable and want to develop in the future. 

If you ask me how it was for me, the onboarding and training process was a moment where I could use to understand the business from all the different areas, where I met my teammates in a relaxed way, in a learning environment and where I discovered the HR area, I fell in love with what we do here, I asked for an opportunity to try something different, and here I am, doing this interview with you today.” 

1.  **\*\*If someone wants more info about the position\*\***

“As I mentioned at the beginning of this meeting, this first instance is to meet you for the first time, to put a face to applications that we think may fit the profile and to provide you with general information about the company so you can also start to get to know us better. 

I understand the enthusiasm to learn more about the position you applied for and its specifics, but if I were to go job by job, this meeting would take too long and that is not the idea. This is why everything related to the role you applied for and the specifics of the position will be briefed in the second round of interviews which is a 1:1, 100% role related meeting with the Head of the Department that corresponds to your role.”

1.  **\*\*If someone won’t stop talking\*\***

We wait for them to finish an idea, then politely say "Thank you for sharing. Let’s move on to (another candidate)."

1.  **\*\*If someone is being disruptive or disrespectful:\*\***

Remove them from the session, it’s better to lose one rude candidate than risk losing a few good ones because they felt disrespected.

1.  **\*\*A candidate asks if the job is paid hourly\*\***

“Our compensation system is a combination of performance based + bonuses and commission. Obviously, the numbers for the commission system vary according to the role, responsibilities and seniority of the position to which each one is applying. It is for this reason that the specific numbers will be given in the 2nd interview which is 100% role related. And remember that this system is for the months of training, because when you grow to other positions, such as management, we move to a salary role.” 

1.  **\*\*What if a candidate applied for an AT\\\&T job add thinking they were going to work directly for them?\*\***

 In these cases, the best thing to do is to quickly explain that AT\&T (or any other company) is one of our clients and that the search was conducted especially to fill vacancies in that account, that's why the company name is put, but if they check well on Indeed, they will see that they applied to our company. 

“Oh, okay, I see where the confusion could be here. AT\&T (or whatever company it is) is one of our clients, and right now, one of the job searches we have open is to fill positions specifically for their account, that's why we nworking for us is that you are not limited to just learning to work for AT\&T but for any amed the client in the job post, because that's how they ask us to do it. While you will be hired and trained by us we maintain the standards of quality and efficiency that our clients require, and the great thing about other company in other industries that require similar skills to the position you applied for.”

1.  **\*\*How old is the Company?\*\***

“This company is relatively new in the area. We have opened a new office in (CITY) and that is why we are conducting this search and hiring process to expand our team in the area. But that doesn't mean we are new in the business,  we are part of a holding company with more than 10 years of experience in marketing, sales and customer service. Our CEO and Founder (NAME) started as a Sales Representative for our sister company and with a lot of effort, hard work and help from his mentors he managed to open his own office, (NAME OF THE COMPANY). And that is what we aspire to with any candidate, that they can project themselves in the long term by opening their own offices and expanding the business model.”

1.  **\*\*Someone asks about the turnover of the company\*\***

“We maintain a turnover rate between 15-20%, which we've achieved through our focus on employee development, competitive benefits, and creating a positive work culture”

1.  **\*\*Someone asks about if the salary is base paid and if it is legal to work 6 days a week on base paid.\*\*** 

"Honestly, I'm just in the talent acquisition team, so I don't know the details about payroll, but I can give a heads up to the manager so they can give you more details in the second rounds"

1.  **\*\*Someone applied to Assistant Manager position but realices is more Entry Level\*\***

“Before reaching a leadership position, it’s crucial that you know the ins and outs of the company, that you experienced first hand what we do and how we do it. That is why everyone that works with us starts at an entry level position and moves forward through the Management Training program. We only promote from with in, so any leadership role starts entry level”
