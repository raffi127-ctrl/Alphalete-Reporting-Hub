Set up Lucy Reports

Takes about five minutes. You will not need to do anything afterwards.

1

Open Terminal

Hold down the Command key and tap Space. A search bar appears in the middle of the screen.

⌘+Space

Type terminal and press Return.

🔍 terminal
>_ Terminal  —  Application

A black window opens. That is Terminal. It is supposed to look like that — nothing is wrong.

2

Copy your line

Press the button. Nothing visible happens — that is normal, it is held ready to paste.

loading…
3

Paste it into the black window and press Return

Click once anywhere inside the black window first, so it is the window you are typing into. Then:

⌘+V thenReturn
$ curl -fsSL …

A lot of text will scroll past for a few minutes. Leave it alone and let it finish — do not close the window.

4

Answer the boxes that pop up

Your SaraPlus login, your OwnerVille login, and where you would like things posted in Slack. That is everything it asks.

A browser window may open by itself with a big red bar. That is Lucy signing in. Leave it completely alone — do not type your password and do not touch the security box. It closes by itself.
Your passwords never leave your computer. They are saved on your Mac and used to sign in from it. Only the report counts are sent.
