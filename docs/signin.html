Sign Lucy back in

Takes about a minute. You will need your My Service Cloud password and your authenticator.

1
Do this on the office computer that runs LucyECO — the one you set up. Signing in on your phone, or in your own browser, will not reach her.
2
Open Terminal: hold Command and tap Space, type terminal, press Return.
3
Press the button, then paste into that window and press Return.
loading…
4
If a box asks for your My Service Cloud email and password, fill it in. That only happens the first time.
5
A browser window opens on My Service Cloud. Sign in there with your authenticator code. It closes by itself and tells you it worked.
Nothing has been lost. Your contracts are all still there — Lucy just cannot see them until she is signed in again. Once she is, the numbers catch up within a few minutes.
