Let Lucy send your background checks

About 7 minutes, one time only. After this, your office admin can press Send background checks and Lucy sends the Sterling invites from this computer. Your Sterling password is saved only on this computer, Only Sterling's emails get forwarded: login codes to the reporting team, results to Lucy's own results inbox. The rest of your inbox stays private.

10 sec

  • Press ⌘ Cmd + Space, type Terminal, and press Return.

6

Save one filter

30 sec

Gmail opens by itself with Sterling's code emails already filled in (noreply@us.fadv.co, subject Authentication Request).

  • Click Create filter.
  • Check Forward it to and pick our address.
  • Click Create filter again, then OK on the small window.

If our address isn't in the list yet, wait a minute and reload the page.

7

Add Lucy's results inbox

1 min

So your admin can see who has passed, Sterling's background check results go to Lucy's own results inbox, lucysterlingreports@gmail.com. They never go to the reporting team.

  • Gmail opens the forwarding settings again. Click Add a forwarding address.
  • Paste with ⌘ Cmd + V. We already copied lucysterlingreports@gmail.com for you.
  • Click Next, Proceed and OK, then OK on the small window.
  • Leave “Disable forwarding” selected, same as before.
8

Save the results filter

30 sec

Gmail opens a box with Sterling's address filled in, and Doesn't have set to “Authentication Request”. That keeps your login codes out of it.

  • Click Create filter.
  • Check Forward it to and pick lucysterlingreports@gmail.com.
  • Click Create filter again, then OK on the small window.
9

Let Lucy test the sign-in

about 1 min

Lucy signs in to Sterling once, types the emailed code herself, and stops. Nothing is sent. Keep the computer open until Terminal says it's done.
